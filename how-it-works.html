How It Works ✨

Lost something? No stress. Here's how it works 👀.

1. Report an Item

Report a lost or found item by filling out the required details.

2. Browse Items

Browse reported lost and found items to look for your missing item.

3. Check the Details

Check the item details, location, date, and status to find a match.

4. Contact

If you find a matching item, use the provided contact information to get in touch with the person who reported it.
